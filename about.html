What is Kylow?

Kylow is a standalone general-purpose AI platform being built as one coherent identity across supported endpoints—not a game-development AI and not a component of THE LONG AFTER.

General purpose

Kylow is intended to support useful work across research, planning, coding, creation and connected tools.

One identity

Desktop, mobile and future endpoints are ways to reach the same Kylow.

Human authority

The user remains the authority over permissions and sensitive actions.

A familiar character

The brand draws warmth from Kylo the dog without claiming the software is literally a dog or a conscious being.
