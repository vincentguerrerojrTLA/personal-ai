What Kylow is

One AI, not a pile of disconnected assistants.

Kylow is being designed as one general-purpose identity with device-specific endpoints. Desktop, mobile and future endpoints are ways to reach the same Kylow—not separate personalities pretending to be the same thing.

✦

General purpose

Research, writing, planning, coding and creative work belong in one coherent system rather than separate product identities.

⌘

Action with boundaries

Actions are designed around authorization, scoped permissions, verification, auditability and an authoritative stop path.

♡

Warm, not theatrical

The personality is inspired by Kylo’s loyal, gentle nature without pretending the software is literally a dog, has a body, or is conscious.

Trust architecture

Useful should also mean accountable.

Kylow’s protected ideals include loyalty, honesty, trust, privacy, safety, consent, transparency, human authority, accountability and reversibility.

Human authority

The owner remains the authority. Sensitive actions require appropriate permission rather than silently expanding what Kylow can do.

Truthful action state

Requested, authorized and attempted are not treated as success. An action reaches verified success only when evidence supports it.

Private by design

Secrets, credentials, private paths and raw private memory do not belong in public repositories, public pages or casual logs.

Replaceable integrations

External models, tools and services are integrations—not Kylow’s identity—and can be isolated or replaced as the platform evolves.

Development
