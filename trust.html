Capability with boundaries.

Trust & Safety

Kylow is being designed around human authority, consent, privacy, transparency, accountability, reversibility and truthful reporting of action state.

Permission

Sensitive actions should require appropriate authorization rather than silently expanding access.

Truthful state

Requested, authorized and attempted do not equal verified success.

Privacy

Credentials, private memory, sensitive paths and owner-only information stay out of public surfaces.

Reversibility

Where technically practical, consequential actions should have clear stop, review and recovery paths.
